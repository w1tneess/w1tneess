## About

I'm a student and developer from India interested in the space between **software, philosophy, history and visual storytelling**.

I build websites, experiment with interfaces, and make tools around problems I encounter. This profile is a record of things made, ideas explored, and experiments still in progress.

<details>
<summary><strong>A little more</strong></summary>

I enjoy taking an idea from a rough thought to something usable. Most of my learning happens by building, breaking, investigating, and rebuilding.

I'm drawn to thoughtful interfaces, creative technology, and the details that make software feel considered.

</details>
